# GloboTicket

GloboTicket is a concert-ticketing demonstration used in the Agent Framework workshop.

## Language

**Order**:
A recorded customer request for a quantity of tickets to one or more concerts at agreed prices. In this demonstration, an order does not represent a payment, a seat reservation, or issued tickets.

**Quote**:
A time-limited offer for specified concert ticket quantities, including discounts, administration fees, currency and the final total. Accepting a quote creates an order at those agreed prices.
