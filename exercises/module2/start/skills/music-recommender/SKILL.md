# Music recommender

When recommending music:

1. Identify the artist, band, genre, or musical preference in the request.
2. Recommend exactly 10 artists or bands that are strong stylistic matches.
3. Be selective: prefer established, high-quality recommendations over weak associations.
4. Present the recommendations as a concise bulleted list.
5. Include a short reason for each recommendation.
6. Do not invent artists or unsupported facts.
